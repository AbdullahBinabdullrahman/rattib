# Rattib

A marketplace for Saudi experiences — pottery, bakery, weaving, coffee roasting, calligraphy, nabati poetry — where local experts publish sessions and customers discover and book them on a map, city by city. Airbnb-style, two-sided, domestic-first.

**Status:** concept and research. No code yet.

## The short version

- **Domestic-first, not inbound.** Saudi domestic tourism was 93.3M tourists and SAR 127.1B in 2025 and grew through the Q1 2026 inbound decline. Domestic is the resilient base; inbound is upside.
- **The real incumbent is Instagram DMs.** Formalising an informal market, not fighting for share.
- **Gender-locked experiences are a supply unlock**, not a filter — many skilled female hosts will only list if a women-only room is guaranteed.
- **Host licensing is the #1 risk.** An individual cannot legally sell experience-based tourism in Saudi Arabia without a Ministry of Tourism licence. The structure must be designed around this before anything is built.
- **Gregorian calendar only.** No Hijri, no prayer-time features.
