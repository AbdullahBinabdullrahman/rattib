# Rattib

A marketplace for Saudi experiences — pottery, bakery, weaving, coffee roasting, calligraphy, nabati poetry — where local experts publish sessions and customers discover and book them on a map, city by city. Airbnb-style, two-sided, domestic-first.

**Status:** concept and research. No code yet.

## The short version

- **Domestic-first, not inbound.** Saudi domestic tourism was 93.3M tourists and SAR 127.1B in 2025 and grew through the Q1 2026 inbound decline. Domestic is the resilient base; inbound is upside.
- **The real incumbent is Instagram DMs.** Formalising an informal market, not fighting for share.
- **Gender-locked experiences are a supply unlock**, not a filter — many skilled female hosts will only list if a women-only room is guaranteed.
- **Every expert needs an economic identity — CR or freelance certificate.** The freelance certificate (`freelance.sa`) is the default: free, instant, no CR required, and its handicrafts and productive-families programmes fit our hosts directly. CR is the upgrade path.
- **Host licensing is still the #1 risk area.** Economic identity is not the same as the right to practise a regulated activity. Activity licences are category-specific, which is why launch scope is crafts and culinary — not guided tours.
- **Gregorian calendar only.** No Hijri, no prayer-time features.
- **Fill rate is the metric the company lives on.** The host gives up 15% commission and must get back materially more income through higher occupancy — modelled at +32%. If the uplift is under 10 points of fill rate, hosts go back to Instagram and the model fails.
